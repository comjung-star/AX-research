# Controlled improvement cycle

This is exploratory development, not a new independent test. No paid calls or Test15 access are permitted. Original files and labels remain unchanged.

First diagnose the final scorer on all 112 cases with stored query/branch evidence. Freeze five conditions before inspecting results: original E, no functional-rule contribution, original-query rules only, no branch semantic contribution, mean rather than maximum branch semantic contribution. Single-query Dense is the required simple baseline. The diagnostic removes components; it does not replace the professor architecture or establish a deployable model.

Use the frozen 2240 working-transfer grades and separately the original 2240 grades for sensitivity. Do not choose the label version that favors a method. Average repeats within query, then macro-average the same eligible queries. Report P@5, F1@5, nDCG@10 and query-paired bootstrap intervals versus E and Dense. Record all conditions including negative results. Intervals after exploratory selection are descriptive, not confirmatory.

Next priorities: candidate preservation with equal budgets; branch semantic preservation; cross-language matching. Every new cycle must state its causal hypothesis and finite variant list before scoring. A positive development result requires improvement over E and Dense on both F1 and nDCG under the primary frozen labels, without reversing the conclusion under sensitivity labels. Even a positive result needs fresh independent evaluation before a paper-level superiority claim. If a cycle has no positive result, diagnose and move to the next priority rather than changing labels or running unlimited weight searches.
