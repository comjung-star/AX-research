strategies.txt explicitly authors each case's selected taxonomy numbers and case-specific evidence/uncertainty. Expansion substitutes only the frozen full IDs, checks source case IDs/counts, and serializes CSV; it does not infer labels or classify by keywords.

Boundaries used: S1 requires a delivered organizational knowledge retrieval function, not merely an internal RAG ingredient for creating a story. S2 requires a delivered user assistance/QA/dialogue function, not the word agent/copilot alone or an authoring prompt. S3 includes existing-source extraction, classification, summaries and image descriptions; simple summaries are not automatically S7. S4 requires an evidenced state prediction, inspection or detection function. S5 requires a concrete selection, allocation, pricing or control support function, not generic usefulness for a decision. S6 requires evidenced business action execution or coordinated process steps; read-only queries, ordinary conversational escalation and generic system integration alone do not establish it. S7 denotes new business text/images (such as support emails, story drafts, translated articles or follow-up drafts), not every generated answer in a conversation. Multiple labels denote separately evidenced delivered functions, not each hidden technical component. ABSTAIN is used where implementation/function detail is insufficient.

Uncertainty is explicit in every rationale. Supplier numbers, projections and combined-workflow benefits are not treated as independently verified causal outcomes. Missing observed outcomes do not prevent labeling a clearly specified implementation, and a benefit number cannot by itself establish a strategy.
